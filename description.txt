Sound Captions 为 Terraria 的音效和当前背景音乐显示字幕。按 F9 编辑字幕位置、大小与单项音量（0%～200%），或在模组配置中集中管理。设置仅保存在本机。

Sound Captions adds captions for sound effects and current background music. Press F9 to edit the layout and adjust individual volumes (0%–200%), or manage saved adjustments in Mod Config. Settings are stored locally.
